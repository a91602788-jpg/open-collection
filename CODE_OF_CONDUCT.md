# Code of conduct / Правила общения

Open Collection welcomes factual and respectful contributions from people of different backgrounds and levels of experience.

- Discuss projects and evidence, not contributors' personal characteristics.
- No harassment, personal attacks, hate speech, doxxing, spam or repeated unsolicited promotion.
- State conflicts of interest when proposing a product with which you are associated.
- Respect copyright, attribution and project ownership.
- Report inappropriate behavior privately to the repository maintainer where possible; do not publish other people's personal information.

Maintainers may edit or remove disruptive comments and decline contributions that violate these standards. Participation does not grant commit or administrative privileges.
