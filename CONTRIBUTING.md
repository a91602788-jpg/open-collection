# Contributing / Как участвовать

Thanks for helping make this index useful. Спасибо за вклад в каталог.

## Suggest a resource / Предложить ресурс

1. Search the [catalog](README.md) to avoid duplicates. / Сначала проверьте, нет ли ссылки в каталоге.
2. Choose **one** primary category. / Выберите одну основную категорию.
3. Open a pull request adding one Markdown bullet under the relevant heading, in alphabetical order. / Добавьте пункт в нужный раздел в алфавитном порядке и создайте PR.
4. Use this format: `- [Project](https://github.com/owner/repository) — Short, factual description.` / Краткое фактическое описание без рекламы.
5. Include the canonical source, explain why it belongs, and report any known limitations or commercial restrictions in the PR description. / Укажите первоисточник, пользу и известные ограничения.

### Acceptance criteria

- The resource is publicly accessible and useful within the chosen category.
- It is described accurately without unsupported statements about security, license, maintenance, cost or production readiness.
- The URL is HTTPS, canonical and free of referral or tracking parameters.
- No duplicate of an existing entry; no copied blocks of someone else's list.
- No malware, phishing, credential harvesting, pirated material, unsolicited promotion or misleading claims.

Commercially licensed or source-available products may appear when clearly described; **not every listing is open source or free**. The maintainers can decline entries for quality or scope reasons. For reports of broken links, use the issue template.

### Rights for contributions

By submitting original editorial descriptions to this repository, you agree to contribute them under [CC0 1.0](LICENSE). You must have the right to submit the text. This grant does **not** cover third-party software, source code, images, trademarks or their licensing terms.

### Maintenance

Please keep changes focused. Do not modify dozens of unrelated sections in one PR. No secrets or private credentials. External links and descriptions are reviewed by maintainers; automated formatting checks are not security audits.
