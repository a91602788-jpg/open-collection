# Community guidelines

Discuss the project and its evidence respectfully. Critique ideas and implementations without harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing or spam. Disclose affiliation and commercial interests when recommending a resource.

Maintainers may remove abusive content and restrict participation. For sensitive concerns, request a private contact channel without posting personal information. These rules apply to this repository’s issues, discussions and pull requests; they do not imply authority over unrelated communities.

Обсуждайте проекты по существу, уважайте участников, раскрывайте заинтересованность в рекомендациях. Не публикуйте личные данные, секреты и спам.
