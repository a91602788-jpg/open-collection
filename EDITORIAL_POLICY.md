# Editorial policy / Редакционная политика

Open Collection is a curated directory of links, not a software distributor, audit provider or endorsement service.

## What belongs here

- Source repositories, frameworks, developer tools, design assets, open educational resources, APIs, business software, self-hosted applications, and worthwhile directories.
- Official upstream URLs whenever available. Public URLs to paid or closed-source resources may be included when their nature is clear.
- Descriptions that explain the *specific function* of a project, rather than promotional language.

## What does not belong

- Referral and affiliate links; tracking query strings; link shorteners; copied third-party curated lists presented as original material.
- Malicious software, phishing, credential theft, pirated or unauthorized resources, deceptive products, or resources without a meaningful public reference.
- Unverifiable claims such as “secure”, “production ready”, “100% free”, “MIT licensed” or “actively maintained” without appropriate evidence.

## Verification boundaries

A syntactically valid link is **not** a verified product. URL existence, ownership of the upstream repository, upstream license, pricing, maintenance, security and deployment suitability are different checks. Unless an entry explicitly links to supporting evidence, treat those attributes as **not independently verified by this collection**. Readers must review primary sources and terms before installation or commercial use.

## Editorial workflow

1. Submit a focused suggestion or PR with the primary upstream URL.
2. Check duplicates, relevance, suspicious redirects and description accuracy.
3. Review upstream licensing/trademark use where content is copied or adapted; prefer original one-line descriptions.
4. Merge only after maintainer review; no automatic publication from external suggestions.
5. Correct, move or remove obsolete links when reported.

## Independence

No sponsored placement, paid ranking, affiliate monetization or claim of affiliation with listed companies. Names, logos, copyrights and licenses belong to their respective owners. If this policy changes, disclose it openly before accepting sponsorship.

## Language policy

Keep English and Russian README navigation synchronized. Category listing descriptions are primarily in English until community-maintained translations are available.
